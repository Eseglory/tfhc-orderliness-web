# Recurring services and reminders

The supplied timetable is stored as ten weekly templates in `service_schedules`. Meetings are generated 28 days ahead on API startup and daily at midnight Africa/Lagos. The unique `(serviceScheduleId, startTime)` key prevents duplicate occurrences. The seeded venue is The Father's House Church, 90 Alagbole–Akute Road, Iju, Ojodu, Ogun State, at latitude 6.6697906 / longitude 3.3581822 (destination coordinates from the supplied Maps link).

Administrators open **Meetings → Configure recurring services and reminders** (`/admin/services`) to add/edit/disable sessions, set start/end times, and change venue, arrival and reminder settings. Children’s Church has three independent sessions and no invented end times; admins may disable/add sessions or supply end times. Until an end time is set, attendance check-in closes ten minutes after the service starts. The default expected arrival is 30 minutes early, with a 100-metre check-in radius; both are editable.

Occurrence times use Africa/Lagos (UTC+01:00), independent of the server timezone. Scheduled instances are not automatically activated for attendance. Editing a template updates its future scheduled instances, cancels obsolete slots without deleting their records, and preserves active/completed instances. Re-enabling restores slots cancelled by the scheduler, but preserves manually cancelled slots.

The saved reminder policy is one hour before each service, to every ACTIVE approved email linked to an ACTIVE member, including approved members who have not yet signed in. Admins can switch reminders off or restrict recipients to members who committed. The SMTP job runs every minute and catches up reminders delayed up to 15 minutes. Each email has a unique delivery claim in `communication_deliveries`, preventing simultaneous workers or repeat runs from sending the same reminder twice. Failed/ambiguous SMTP attempts are recorded and require review rather than automatic retries; a process crash after claiming can leave a PENDING record for review. Cancellations and approval status are checked before sending.

Seed with `node scripts/seed-recurring-services.cjs --apply`. Re-running preserves administrator edits and existing instances. The seed sends no email. Templates, the next 40 instances and the one-hour policy were saved to the configured database. To run automated reminders in production, deploy the API/web changes and set the API's `SMTP_PASSWORD` secret in Render (the workspace does not have Render API credentials). No reminder emails were sent during setup.

Validation: four scheduling/deduplication unit tests; a real PostgreSQL lifecycle test covering creation, repeat generation, rescheduling, disabling/re-enabling and claimed delivery with mocked SMTP; desktop and mobile browser tests covering session configuration. API/web type checks and production builds passed. The browser and integration fixtures use only the isolated local test database.
