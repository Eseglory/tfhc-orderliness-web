## Description

TFHC Orderliness helps approved members of The Father’s House Church stay connected and ready to serve.

Use the app to view upcoming meetings, check in for attendance, share your weekly service availability, and receive important service reminders. Your church team can coordinate participation while members have a clear view of their attendance and commitments.

TFHC Orderliness is intended for authorised church members and workers.
